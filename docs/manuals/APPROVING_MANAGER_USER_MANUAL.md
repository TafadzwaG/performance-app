# Approving Manager User Manual

## Purpose

This guide is for approving managers who perform the final business review before HR finalization.

## What You Are Responsible For

- reviewing manager-completed appraisals
- validating scoring consistency and comment quality
- approving strong, complete submissions
- sending back weak or incomplete submissions
- recording final approval-stage comments

## Approval Checklist

Before approving, confirm that:

- the appraisal has reached the approval stage
- required objectives and competencies are rated
- comments are clear and professional
- the overall result makes sense
- any send-back history has been resolved

## When To Send Back

Use send-back when:

- evidence is missing
- ratings are inconsistent with comments
- important objectives are incomplete
- development actions are unclear

## Good Practice Tips

- focus on quality and consistency, not data entry
- explain send-back reasons precisely
- avoid approving records with unresolved gaps
