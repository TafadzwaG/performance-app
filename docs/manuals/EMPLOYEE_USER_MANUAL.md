# Employee User Manual

## Purpose

This guide is for employees who plan goals, complete self assessments, upload evidence, and review development actions.

## What You Are Responsible For

- completing your employee profile if required
- planning objectives with correct total weight
- entering performance achieved
- submitting self ratings and comments on time
- attaching evidence where relevant
- reviewing send-back requests and correcting entries
- following agreed development actions

## Your Main Pages

- `Dashboard`
- `Appraisals`
- `Development Plans`
- `Help & Docs`

## Typical Employee Workflow

### Complete your profile

If the system redirects you to profile completion after sign-in, fill in all required employment and contact details first.

### Plan your goals

During goal-setting:

- review the assigned cycle
- confirm the template structure
- add or refine objectives
- make sure weights total `100`

### Submit self assessment

When self assessment opens:

- update performance achieved
- choose self ratings
- add achievement notes and significant issues
- attach evidence or links
- submit once complete

### Respond to send-backs

If a manager sends the appraisal back:

- open the appraisal again
- read comments carefully
- correct the required sections
- resubmit

### Review the outcome

After approval and finalization:

- review final scores and comments
- read the development plan
- download the print or PDF summary if needed

## Good Practice Tips

- keep objective titles specific and measurable
- provide evidence early instead of waiting until deadlines
- explain underperformance clearly and factually
- avoid submitting until all sections are complete
