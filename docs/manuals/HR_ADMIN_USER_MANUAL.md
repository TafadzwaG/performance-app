# HR Admin User Manual

## Purpose

This guide is for HR administrators who manage setup data, employee readiness, cycles, reporting, and finalization.

## What You Are Responsible For

- maintaining setup masters
- linking users to employee profiles
- assigning reporting lines
- creating and opening review cycles
- assigning employees and templates
- monitoring workflow completion
- finalizing approved appraisals
- exporting reports and documentation

## Operational Areas

### Setup

Maintain:

- departments
- job titles
- perspectives
- competencies
- rating scales

### Employees

Ensure employee profiles are complete and managers are linked correctly.

### Review cycles

Create cycles with clear deadlines and track their open or closed state.

### Reports and outputs

Use report pages to monitor:

- completion status
- overdue reviews
- rating distribution
- department or employee summaries

### Finalization

Finalize only after approval is complete. Finalization should lock the record and make final output packs available.

## Good Practice Tips

- keep setup catalogues tidy and active states accurate
- prevent missing approvers before cycle assignment
- review overdue queues frequently
- use exported summaries for governance and planning meetings
